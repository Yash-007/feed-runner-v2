# Feed Runner — visual redesign, fly.io inspired

## What actually makes that site look the way it does

Nine decisions, in rough order of how much each one carries:

  1. Serif display headings, near black, large, tight tracking, paired with a
     rounded geometric sans for body text in a muted purple grey. That pairing is
     most of the identity.
  2. Very light pastel gradient fields: mint into white into blush into lavender,
     all desaturated, with a fine halftone dot texture over them.
  3. Flat. No drop shadows anywhere in the content.
  4. Separation by 1px hairline only. Cards are bordered rectangles, not filled
     tiles.
  5. One saturated accent, violet, used sparingly: the primary button and the
     round nav buttons. Everything else is ink, grey, or pastel.
  6. Big radius on the outer panel, small radius inside it.
  7. Primary buttons are small radius rectangles, deliberately not pills. The
     round buttons are for icons only, and the contrast between the two is on
     purpose.
  8. Horizontal rails that bleed off the right edge, so you can see there is more
     without a scrollbar.
  9. Generous whitespace, low density.

We already share one thing with it: a violet accent. Everything else is ours to
change.

Not copying: the hand drawn illustrations. There is no artist, and generated
art would look worse than none.


## Foundation

  [x] F1  Bundle a serif for display and a sans for body, build a Typography that
          uses serif for display/headline and sans for everything else
  [x] F2  Repaint the palette: ink text, muted purple grey secondary text, a real
          hairline colour, violet kept as the single accent
  [x] F3  A Wash composable: pastel gradient plus optional halftone dots, with a
          dark mode counterpart, for header bands
  [x] F4  One card style: hairline border, no fill, no elevation, generous padding.
          Elevation survives only where something really floats, the sheet itself
          and the selection toolbar
  [x] F5  One button set: primary solid violet small radius, secondary tinted,
          round icon button. Defined once, used everywhere
  [x] F6  A spacing scale, applied instead of the current ad hoc numbers


## Our own screens, where the design can go all in

  [x] S1  Setup: serif "Feed Runner" on a wash header, readiness lines as one
          bordered card split by hairlines, voice rules as a bordered field,
          restyled buttons
  [x] S2  Ideas: serif "Ideas" on a wash header, streak card flat with a hairline,
          filter chips squared off with hairline when unselected and solid violet
          when selected, seed cards bordered instead of filled, bottom bar flat
          with a top hairline
  [x] S3  Seed thread: seed headline in serif on a wash band, idea cards bordered,
          composer bordered, instruction bubbles keep the violet tint


## Overlay panels, where legibility over X comes first

  [x] S4  Reply sheet: a thin wash band behind the header only, body left plain.
          Draft cards bordered, angle chips squared off
  [x] S5  Saved results strip becomes a rail: vertical hairlines between entries,
          bleeding off the right edge
  [x] S6  Compose sheet: same treatment, and Post/Quote becomes a proper segmented
          control like the toggle in the reference hero
  [x] S7  Bubble menu pills: flat, hairline, smaller radius, accent dots kept


## Decisions

  - Type: Fraunces for display, Figtree for body. Warm high contrast serif with a
    soft axis, rounded geometric sans, the closest pair to the reference.
  - Gradients: full on our own screens, a thin band behind overlay headers only.
    Reply text sits over X and needs its contrast.
  - Angle chips: pale fills with darker text of the same hue. The one place we do
    not follow the single accent rule, because the colour is doing real work.
